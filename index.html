Simple to store. Easy to serve.

Keep your Lamilicious food frozen until your pet is ready to eat.

🧊 How long does it last?

  • Keep frozen for up to 3 months until use.
  • Once defrosted, keep refrigerated.
  • Use within 3 days after defrosting.
  • Keep refrigerated between servings.

🍽️ How to prepare

STEP 01

Defrost

Defrost at room temperature or overnight in the refrigerator.

STEP 02

Serve

Serve at room temperature or chilled.

STEP 03

No heating

Heating the food is not recommended.
